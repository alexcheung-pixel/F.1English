Grammar Quick Guide

5 Target Parts of Speech (F.1 Level)

1. Noun (名詞) Who / What

Names of people, places, things, or jobs. Look for "a", "an", "the" in front!

Examples: principal, lab technician, janitor, school, book, run (as a noun: "go for a run")
2. Verb (動詞) Action / State

Describes an action, habit, or state (what someone does or experiences).

Examples: collects, trains, listen, eat, decorated, lasted, snapped
3. Adjective (形容詞) Describes Noun

Describes or gives more detail about a noun (How is it? What kind?).

Examples: delicious cake, blue uniform, kind teacher, dangerous experiment, fast runner
4. Adverb (副詞) How / How much

Describes HOW an action is done (often ends in -ly) or modifies an adjective.

Examples: runs quickly, writes neatly, speak politely, arrived late, pretty big
5. Preposition (介詞) Time / Location

Shows position, place, direction, or time connection.

Examples: under the desk, on the wall, in the evening, beside, for 10 years, by Monday
