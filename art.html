Grammar Cheat Sheet (冠詞秘笈)

Reference notes based on F.1 English curriculum

1 Indefinite Articles: "a" vs "an"

Used before singular, countable nouns when introducing them for the first time.

  • Use "a" before consonant sounds (輔音發音): e.g., a car, a teacher, a student, a BBC film (/b/ sound)
  • Use "an" before vowel sounds (a, e, i, o, u 元音發音): e.g., an actor, an orange, an essay, an MTR station (/em/ sound)
  • ⚠️ Pronunciation Traps (特例考點!):
    • a university → Starts with a "Y" consonant sound (/juː/).
    • an hour → Silent 'h'! Starts with a vowel sound (/aʊər/).

2 Definite Article: "the"

  • 1. Unique objects (獨一無二事物): the sun, the moon, the world, the Earth, the Hong Kong Museum of Art
  • 2. Second Mention (再次提及): Gary works in a studio. The studio is in Kwun Tong.
  • 3. Known / Specified item (特定事物): The books on the shelf; The girl in front of Joe

3 Zero Article: "X" (不加冠詞)

  • Proper Nouns / Cities: Hong Kong, Thailand, Paris.
  • Sports & Games: play basketball, play football.
  • General Uncountable Nouns: drink milk, eat rice.
  • General Plural Nouns: Dogs make great pets.
