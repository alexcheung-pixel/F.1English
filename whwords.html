Mastering Wh-Questions

Learn question words, targeted information, What vs. Which rules, Wh + noun phrases, sentence structures, and Subject vs. Object questions with colorful interactive visuals!

What Things / Actions

Asks about objects, actions, ideas, or information.

e.g. "What did you do in Japan during Christmas?"
When Time

Asks about time, dates, days, or schedules.

e.g. "When will the school choir perform?"
Where Places

Asks about location, position, or direction.

e.g. "Where are you having lunch in Mong Kok?"
Why Reasons

Asks for reasons or causes (often answered with 'Because').

e.g. "Why didn't you take a boat trip in Sai Kung?"
Who People

Asks about human subjects or objects.

e.g. "Who did you go to Ocean Park with?"
Whose Possession

Asks about who owns an item or object.

e.g. "Whose camera is this on the teacher's desk?"
Which Choices (List)

Asks to choose from a known limited list of options.

e.g. "Which tourist attraction do you like better?"
How Manner / Means

Asks about method, way, condition, or process.

e.g. "How did you book the MTR tickets?"

The HK Curriculum Rule: What vs Which

Both words ask about choices, but have specific guidelines in S1 HK Exams:

WHICH Known / Limited Options
  • Used when we know the exact number of choices.
  • Used when choosing directly from a listed option.
👉 "Which T-shirt do you like – the red one or the green one?"
WHAT Open / Unlimited Set
  • Used when options are open-ended or unlimited.
  • Note: In casual conversation, either can sometimes be used (e.g., What/Which festivals do you like?).
👉 "What is your favourite school subject?"

What / Which / Whose + Noun or Noun Phrase

We often attach a noun directly after the Wh-word to specify the exact thing being asked about:

What time did you go to bed?
Which TV channels do you watch?
Whose backpack is this?
A

Wh-word + Verb 'To Be'

1. Wh-word + be + subject Where is Legoland Park?
2. Wh-word + be + possessive pronoun Which bag is yours?
3. Wh-word + be + adjective Who was late for assembly?
4. Wh-word + be + subject + -ing Why are we dining here?
B

Wh-words + Auxiliary Verbs (Do / Does / Did)

1. Present Tense Formula:
Wh-word + do/does + subject + base verb
  • What do you want for dinner?
  • When does she come to school?
2. Past Tense Formula:
Wh-word + did + subject + base verb
  • What did you do last night?
  • Where did they go last year?

Subject vs Object Wh-Questions

Some Wh-words (like who, what, which, whose) can be either the Subject or Object of the question!

Subject Question NO Auxiliary do/does/did

The Wh-word acts as the subject (the doer of the action).

Wh-word + main verb + object
👉 "Who destroyed the window?"
(Answer: Tom destroyed it. 'Tom' is subject!)
Object Question REQUIRES do/does/did

The Wh-word acts as the object receiving the action.

Wh-word + do/does/did + subject + main verb
👉 "What does Tony like?"
(Answer: Tony likes pizza. 'Pizza' is object!)
